Junior Cricket

Where the club
starts.

Junior cricket at Eden Roskill runs from Under-11 through to Under-19, offering structured pathways in the Auckland Cricket Association age-grade competitions. We provide professional coaching, quality equipment, and a welcoming environment for every young cricketer.

We believe junior cricket should be fun first, building skills, confidence, and friendships that last a lifetime. Many of our Premier Men started in these junior programs.

Development Pathway

Age-grade competition

U11

Under-11, Fundamentals

Modified cricket introducing the basics of batting, bowling, and fielding. Focus on fun, participation, and developing a love of the game.

U13

Under-13, Development

Age-grade competition in the ACA Junior competitions. Players develop core skills and begin learning team tactics and game awareness.

U15

Under-15, Competition

Full ACA competition format. Players are introduced to the demands of the game, longer formats, strategy, and the importance of preparation.

U17

Under-17, Representative pathway

High-level junior competition. Strong performers are encouraged to trial for Auckland age-group representative sides.

U19

Under-19, Senior transition

The bridge to senior cricket. U19s experience the culture and expectations of the club and are introduced to senior players and coaches.

Qualified Coaching

Our junior coaches hold NZC accreditations and are committed to player development at every level.

Senior Cricket

Five sides.
Every level.

Eden Roskill fields multiple senior teams across the Auckland Cricket Association competitions, from Premier Men at the top level through to club grade and social sides. Whatever your standard, there is a team for you.

Senior cricket at ERDCC runs October to March with training starting in September. New players are always welcome, come to a training session and we'll find the right team for you.

Indoor Training

Train all year.
Stay sharp.

The off-season doesn't have to mean no cricket. Eden Roskill offers bookable indoor net sessions and structured skills coaching throughout the year at our indoor facilities.

Whether you're preparing for the upcoming season, working on a specific skill, or just keeping your eye in, our nets are available for individuals, groups, and teams.
