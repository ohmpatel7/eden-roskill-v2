Who We Are

Different
backgrounds.
One crest.

Eden Roskill is one of Auckland's most multicultural cricket clubs. From Premier grade to junior development, we welcome players of every background, age, and ability. If you love cricket, there's a place for you here.


Founded in 1903 and based at Keith Hay Park in Mt Roskill, we have been part of the Auckland Cricket Association for over a century. Our community spans dozens of cultures and nationalities, united by one shared passion.


Whether you are chasing your first wicket or your tenth Premier title, Eden Roskill is the club for you. Come as you are. Leave as family.
