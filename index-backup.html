Our Story

More Than
Just Cricket

Eden Roskill Cricket Club has been a cornerstone of Auckland cricket since 1903. Originally established as separate entities, Eden and Roskill merged in 1952 to form one of the most respected clubs in New Zealand cricket.

Today we call Keith Hay Park, Mt Roskill home. Our grounds are a hub of activity every weekend throughout the season, filled with the sound of bat on ball, families cheering, and teammates celebrating together.

We compete across Auckland Cricket Association competitions including Premier Men, T20 grades, and a thriving junior program that nurtures the next generation of New Zealand cricketers.

We are a multicultural, community-first club that welcomes players and supporters from all walks of life. Whether you are a seasoned cricketer or picking up a bat for the first time, you belong here.
